*Where money comes from* starts with a central bank buying a bond. This flow follows what happens when it buys on a huge scale (quantitative easing, QE), and when it lets those bonds run off (quantitative tightening, QT).

| Step | Page |
|---|---|
| The central bank buys government bonds with new reserves | [[central-bank-money]] |
| The sellers' banks hold more reserves, and their customers more deposits | [[bank-deposits]] |
| Bond prices rise and yields fall | [[bond-pricing]] |
| Long-term rates fall and the yield curve flattens | [[yield-curve]] |
| Money looks for a return elsewhere, and liquidity is plentiful | [[liquidity]] |
| In QT, bonds mature without being replaced and reserves shrink | [[central-bank-money]] |
