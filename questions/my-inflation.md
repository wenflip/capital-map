---
id: my-inflation
question: Is the official inflation rate the same as my own?
group: Why do prices rise?
order: 2
---
Not exactly. The official rate tracks the cost of a typical basket of goods and services, averaged across many households. Your own spending is different. If you spend more than average on rent, fuel or food, your costs can rise faster or slower than the official number. The official rate is a useful guide to the whole economy, but your own inflation depends on what you buy.
