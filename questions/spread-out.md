question: Why does spreading my money around matter, and when does it fail?
group: How investing works
order: 5
---
Spreading your money means one bad event can't take all of it. It works when the things you own fail for different reasons: a bank failure, a fall in shares and a rise in prices don't usually hit everything at once. It fails when they fall together. In a crisis, many things that normally move apart can suddenly drop at the same time. So it helps to count what could hurt each thing you own, not just how many things you own.
