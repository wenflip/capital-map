group: How investing works

Saving keeps your money safe and easy to reach, usually in a bank, and it earns a little interest. Investing puts money into things like shares or bonds that can grow more over time. The price of that growth is risk: the value can fall, sometimes a lot, and you may have to wait years for it to recover. Many people do both: savings for the short term, investments for long-term goals.
