---
id: broker-bust
question: What happens to my shares if my broker goes bust?
group: Is my money safe?
order: 2
---
Your shares are not the broker's money. A broker is meant to keep customers' shares apart from its own, so if it fails they can be handed back or moved to another firm. This can take months, and you may not be able to trade while it happens. If shares have gone missing, many countries have a scheme that pays back up to a limit. No scheme pays you back if your shares simply fall in price.
