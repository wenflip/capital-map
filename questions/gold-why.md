Gold has been used as money for thousands of years. Nobody owes it to you, so it can't go bust the way a bank or a company can. People buy it to hold something outside the banking system, to store value over long periods, or because they worry about rising prices or a crisis. The trade-offs are real: it pays no income, it can lose value for decades, and physical gold can be stolen. After its 1980 high, it took until 2008 to pass that price again.
