question: Do I really own the shares in my account?
group: Is my money safe?
order: 4
---
Yes, but usually not directly. Your shares are normally held in your broker's name, or by a firm it uses, on your behalf, and the broker's records show which ones are yours. This is the normal way shares are held. It works well as long as each firm keeps customers' shares apart from its own. What you own is a claim at the end of a chain of firms, so it is worth knowing how your broker holds them.
