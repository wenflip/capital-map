question: What does "leverage" mean, and why does it wipe people out?
group: When things go wrong
order: 3
---
Leverage means investing with borrowed money. It makes gains bigger, and losses bigger too. With twice your own money invested, a 50% fall wipes you out; with ten times, a 10% fall does. Lenders usually sell your investments before that point, often at the worst moment, so you can't wait for prices to recover. That is why falls that other investors sit through can end a borrowed position completely.
