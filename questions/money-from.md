group: How money works

Most money is not printed. It is created when banks make loans: the bank adds the loan to the borrower's account as new money. The central bank makes the base layer, which is notes and the money banks hold with it. Every other kind of money is a promise to pay in that base layer. When a loan is repaid, the money it created disappears again.
