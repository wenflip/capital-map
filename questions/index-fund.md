An index fund buys every company on a list, such as the biggest companies in a market, instead of picking a few. Because no one is paid to choose, it is usually cheap. People recommend them for two reasons. Low fees leave more of the return for you. And in long-running comparisons of US funds, most that tried to beat an index did not manage it once their higher fees were paid. The trade-off is that you get the whole market, falls included, with no chance of beating it.
