---
id: stablecoin-safe
question: Is a stablecoin as safe as a dollar in the bank?
group: Crypto
order: 2
---
No, they work differently. A stablecoin is a company's promise to swap each token for a dollar, backed by whatever reserves it holds. A bank deposit is also a promise, but in many countries a public scheme pays it back up to a limit if the bank fails. Most stablecoins have no such scheme. If people doubt the reserves, the price can slip below a dollar, the same way a run hits a bank.
