question: Is cash a safe place to keep savings?
group: Is my money safe?
order: 3
---
For the short term, mostly yes: the number never goes down. Over many years, prices usually rise, so the same cash buys less and less. At 3% a year, money loses almost half its buying power in 20 years. Cash is good for spending and for a safety buffer, and weak for storing wealth for decades. Cash kept in a bank also carries the bank's risk, which is a separate question.
