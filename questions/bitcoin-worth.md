group: Crypto
order: 1

Bitcoin is a digital coin kept on a public record that thousands of computers share. Nobody owes it to you, so its value can't come from a promise. It is worth something only because people want to hold it and expect others to keep wanting it. Its rules allow no more than 21 million coins, and the network has proved hard to break. Its price can still swing a lot, and most losses come from how people hold it rather than from the system itself.
