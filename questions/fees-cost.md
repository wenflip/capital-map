id: fees-cost
question: How much do fees really cost me over time?
group: How investing works
order: 2
---
More than they look. A fee is taken every year, and the money it removes would have kept growing. So a fee of 1% a year can take a large share of what you end up with after 30 years, not just 1%. The fee is also the one cost you know in advance, which is why it is worth checking before you buy anything.
