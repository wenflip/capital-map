question: Can a country go broke? What happens to people's money if it does?
group: When things go wrong
order: 1
---
Yes. Governments have failed to pay their debts many times in history. A country that borrows in its own currency can usually print money to pay, but then prices may rise fast instead. When a country gets into deep trouble, savers can be hit in several ways at once: high inflation, limits on taking money out, bank deposits frozen, or government bonds paid back at less than promised. Money held in that country's currency and banks is usually hit hardest.
