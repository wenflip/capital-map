Money in the bank is not kept in a box with your name on it. It is a loan from you to the bank, and the bank promises to pay it back when you ask. If a bank fails, most countries have a scheme that pays back each person up to a set limit. Below that limit your money is very safe. Above it, you may have to wait or lose part of it, so it helps to know the limit where you live.
