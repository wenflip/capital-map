question: Can the government take my money or gold?
group: When things go wrong
order: 2
---
It has happened, and it can again. Governments have ordered people to hand in gold, as the US did in 1933, frozen bank accounts, limited how much money can leave the country, and taken over whole companies. This is rare in stable countries, but common across history. Things the state can see and reach, like bank accounts, property and shares in your name, are the easiest to take. That is one reason people spread what they own across different kinds of assets and places.
