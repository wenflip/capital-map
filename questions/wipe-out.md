question: What could wipe out my savings?
group: When things go wrong
order: 4

There is no single answer, because it depends on what you own. Every kind of wealth has something that can kill it: theft for gold, a failed firm for money held with it, rising prices for cash, illness for your ability to earn. The real danger is having everything exposed to the same one. A spread across things that fail for different reasons is much harder to wipe out than any single choice.
