title: Privacy
summary: What The Capital Map stores, what it measures, and what it doesn't.
---
**In short:** no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no tracking cookies. Your reading settings stay in your own browser. Visits are counted with privacy-first analytics that don't identify you.

## What stays in your browser
The site saves a few settings in your browser's local storage, so they're there next time you visit:
- your view settings: depth (Core or Everything) and theme;
- which map branches you left open;
- the pages you've ticked as known;
- whether you've closed the welcome guide.

This information **never leaves your device**. It isn't sent to the site or to anyone else. Clearing your browser's site data for this address removes all of it. Each browser and device keeps its own copy.

## Visit statistics
The site counts visits with **Cloudflare Web Analytics**, a privacy-first service that:
- doesn't use cookies or local storage;
- doesn't build a profile of you or follow you across other sites;
- isn't used for advertising.

It records, in aggregate: which pages are viewed, the page that linked to the site, the browser, operating system and device type, and the country the visit comes from. These figures are used only to see which pages are read and where visitors come from, so the site can be improved.

## Hosting
The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. As with any website, Cloudflare processes technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. This is handled under Cloudflare's own privacy policy; the site doesn't receive or store a list of visitors.

## What the site doesn't do
- No sign-up, login or user accounts.
- No contact or feedback forms that collect personal details.
- No advertising, and no advertising or social-media tracking scripts.
- No selling or sharing of data.
