## What it is
The **capital system** is every way wealth can be stored, moved and grown: money, debts, ownership stakes, physical things, digital tokens, and the skills and relationships inside you. This map lays them out as one connected tree.

## How the map is organised
- **The tree is organised by what things are.** Each branch is a kind of asset or a body of ideas. A page lives in one place on the tree and links to everything else it touches.
- **One question sorts money and claims:** *whose liability is it, and what is it a claim on?* See [[claims-and-liabilities]].
- **One model sorts stores of wealth by risk:** the [[cartography-of-wealth|Cartography of Wealth]], which maps each store by how embodied in you it is and how visible it is to the state, and names the one thing most likely to kill it.
- **How things trade right now** (for example, crypto moving like high-growth tech shares) is a separate question. It changes with conditions, so it lives under [[market-regimes]] rather than shaping the tree.

> **Key rule:** this is a reference, not advice. It explains how things work so you can judge for yourself.
