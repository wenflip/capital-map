## What it is
The **capital system** is every way wealth can be stored, moved and grown: money, debts, ownership stakes, physical things, digital tokens, and the skills and relationships inside you. This map lays them out as one connected tree.

## How the map is organised
- **The tree is organised by what things are.** Each branch is a kind of asset or a body of ideas. A page lives in one place on the tree and links to everything else it touches.
- **One question sorts money and claims:** *whose liability is it, and what is it a claim on?* See [[claims-and-liabilities]].
- **One model sorts stores of wealth by risk:** the [[cartography-of-wealth|Cartography of Wealth]], which maps each store by how embodied in you it is and how visible it is to the state, and names the one thing most likely to kill it.
- **How things trade right now** (for example, crypto moving like high-growth tech shares) is a separate question. It changes with conditions, so it lives under [[market-regimes]] rather than shaping the tree.

## The branches
The branches sit in four groups, shown as clusters on the map.

**Foundations** — the ideas and the money everything else is built on.
- [[foundation]] — claims, time value, compounding, risk and inflation.
- [[money]] — how the money system works: its layers, central banks, interest rates and exchange rates.

**What you can own** — in order from inside you to fully on the record, following the [[cartography-of-wealth|Cartography of Wealth]].
- [[personal-capital]] — skills, health, reputation and network.
- [[digital-assets]] — bearer tokens on public ledgers.
- [[money-you-hold]] — banknotes, bank deposits and gold: the money people actually keep.
- [[real-assets]] — property, commodities and collectibles.
- [[fixed-income]] — lending for a promised return.
- [[equities]] — owning a share of a business.

**Putting it together** — using and combining what you own.
- [[derivatives]] — contracts on other assets, and leverage.
- [[portfolio]] — combining assets so that no single failure clears the board.

**How it runs** — the machinery, the scorecard and the practicalities.
- [[markets]] — exchanges, brokers, custody and settlement.
- [[measure]] — returns, drawdown and volatility.
- [[practice]] — tax, accounts, common mistakes, data and charting.

> **Key rule:** this is a reference, not advice. It explains how things work so you can judge for yourself.
