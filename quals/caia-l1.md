The Chartered Alternative Investment Analyst programme covers the assets outside listed shares and bonds. Topic names follow the CAIA Association's published curriculum; the association has announced new weights from 2027, so check the current outline before an exam. The pages are reference notes in our own words, not the official study material, so use them alongside it.
