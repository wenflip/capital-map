Level III has a core every candidate takes, plus one of three pathways chosen at registration: Portfolio Management, Private Markets or Private Wealth. This map follows the core and the **Portfolio Management** pathway, which is the public-markets machine. Private Wealth is mainly client planning, and Private Markets is mainly the fund manager's view, so both are left out. Check the current outline before an exam.
